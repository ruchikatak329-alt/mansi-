A Little About Me

I'm a content creator who loves the whole journey of a post: the first spark of an idea, the shoot, the edit, and the moment someone stops scrolling because of it.

I started out as a social media trainee at Catchy Climbers, a marketing agency, where I handled a client's page, came up with post and reel concepts and learned how a brand's voice stays consistent across platforms.

Now I create reels and posts for small businesses, cutting raw footage into short videos built to hold attention. I like content that feels warm, a little playful, and always has a reason to exist.

good content feels like a friend showing you something, not a brand telling you something.
